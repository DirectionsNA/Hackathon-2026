# Directions North America 2026 Hackathon

Welcome to the **Directions North America 2026 Hackathon** 🎉  

On **April 29th, 2026**, we'll pause for **90 minutes** to reflect on what we've learned during Directions North America — and turn those insights into new ideas that can shape the future of Business Central and its ecosystem.

---

## 🧠 The Challenge

**Your task:**  
1. Spend 90 minutes reflecting on what inspired you during the conference.  
2. Come up with a **hackathon project idea** — something you (and optionally one teammate) can complete in the next few months.  
3. Submit your idea **by creating a new GitHub Issue** in this repository before **14:30 on April 29th, 2026**.  

You'll then have until **August 1st, 2026** to bring your idea to life.

---

## 🏆 Prize

The **winning team** will receive  
🎫 **Two free tickets to Directions North America 2027!**

---

## 👥 Team Details

- Team size: **1–x people**
- Each person can only be a member of **one team**.
- Each team submits **one idea** via a GitHub Issue.  
- Implementation needs to be Open Source and on GitHub.
- All project submissions **must include a demo video**.

---

## 💡 How to Submit Your Idea

1. Go to the **Issues** tab.  
2. Click **New Issue**.  
3. Choose **“Hackathon Project Idea”**.  
4. Fill out the template — including your reflection, idea, and plan.  
5. Submit it **before 14:30 (local time) on April 29th, 2026.**

---

## 📅 Timeline

| Phase | Dates | Description |
|-------|--------|-------------|
| Reflection & Idea Submission | **Apr 29, 2026 (until 14:30)** | Reflect for 90 minutes and submit your idea as an Issue |
| Development | **Apr 30, 2026 – Jul 31, 2026** | Work on your project |
